Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. 100% On-Device Processing

At DocZen, we believe your documents should stay private. Every operation?whether it's scanning a document, converting images to PDF, or compressing a file?happens entirely on your device. We do not use servers to process your data.

2. No Data Sharing

Since all processing is local, your files never leave your phone. We do not collect, store, or share your personal documents or images with any third party or cloud service.

3. Permissions We Request

Camera: Required for the Smart Scanner feature to capture document images.

Storage: Required to read PDF files from your device and save the processed results back to your Downloads folder

Internet: Required for app dependencies and AdMob services to show rewarded ads.

4. Monetization & Ads

DocZen is a completely free application. We use Google AdMob to show 'Rewarded Ads'. By watching a single ad, you get 1 hour of uninterrupted, ad-free access to all premium tools. There are no hidden in-app purchases.

5. Security

We provide built-in PDF encryption and decryption tools to help you secure your files with passwords. These passwords are never shared or stored by us.
